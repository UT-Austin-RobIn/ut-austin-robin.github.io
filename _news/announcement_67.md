[Oopsie Data](https://oopsie-data.com/) is now open for contributions! Co-led by Arpit and co-advised by Roberto, with a fantastic [team](https://oopsie-data.com/team/) of researchers across multiple universities, Oopsie Data aims to build a diverse, crowdsourced dataset of robot manipulation failures.
